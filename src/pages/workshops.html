Why this workshop matters

College is one of the largest investments your family will ever make.

What is my student actually preparing for? Most families make this investment before answering that question. Choosing the wrong path can mean:

  • Tens of thousands of dollars in unnecessary debt
  • Multiple major changes and wasted semesters
  • Extra years in college with no clear direction
  • Delayed career success and confidence
  • Stress and financial strain for the entire family

Our process helps families avoid these costly mistakes by starting with the end in mind: the career.
