Choosing the right path after high school is not just about test scores and essays (though they matter too). It’s about finding the path your student was always meant to be on. So we find the why before the where.

How it works

It starts with four easy steps.

Our advisors lead students and parents on a journey of self-discovery that starts in early high school, then build the college strategy and financial plan around what you learn.

  1. Step1

    Complimentary family strategy call

    We start by meeting your family where you are in the college and career journey.

    You can’t plan the path without knowing where you’re starting.

    • Learn about your child’s interests, strengths and aspirations
    • Uncover the common blind spots that derail many students
    • Answer the big question: “What should we do next?”
  2. Step2

    Career exploration & student discovery

    Find the why before choosing the where. 1:1 coaching with an expert advisor, using assessments, exploratory conversations and tools.

    We help your child start with purpose.

    • Discover the career paths aligned to your student’s interests
    • See what those paths look like in real life: education, lifestyle and income
    • Build motivation and clarity so your student owns the process
  3. Step3

    Educational pathway & college strategy

    24 academic strategy calls help your student every step of the way.

    We take the pressure off you and your teen by managing the timeline and guiding every step.

    • Identify colleges, or alternative paths, that support career goals
    • Research schools that fit your student’s academic and financial needs
    • Guide essays, applications, deadlines and decisions
  4. Step4

    Final decision & financial strategy

    Choose with confidence. Plan with clarity. By the end of the journey your family will be ready to decide.

    A clear plan, a confident choice, and peace of mind.

    • Compare acceptances, aid packages and academic programs
    • Understand the short and long-term implications of each choice
    • Make a decision based on both heart and logic
