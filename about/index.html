Our story

Helping students find their best-fit career and college since 2009.

We started Pathfinders, then named College Liftoff, back in 2009: a small group of people passionate about helping high school students find their footing on a pathway to a fulfilling career.

Working with families, we quickly saw the need for help with the college decision itself, especially as college costs rose. College fees have risen more than 200% over the past 25 years, and the trend continues. We saw how a lack of expertise in choosing a school and major cost families thousands of hard-earned dollars, and time, a resource even more irreplaceable.

So we built a service to prevent those losses, with personalized support for students and parents through the entire journey: career planning, college applications and securing financial aid.

Over the years we have adapted, tested, improved and proven our services based on feedback from our students and families, and on the outcomes of the applications they submit with our guidance. We genuinely love what we do and have confidence in the service we offer.
